About the studio

Inspiring innovation, one house at a time.

Marshall Tech automates homes from two studios: Rathinapuri in Coimbatore, and Grace Hill in Coonoor. We welcome customization in that automation.

The work

More than a switch on a phone.

The card says it plainly: LEDs, controllers, sensors, and much more. A finished job is the lighting, the locks, the cameras, the pump, the purifier, the inverter, and the wiring that holds them together.

A scene is only as good as the circuit under it. That is why electrical and plumbing sit on the same list as the automation products. Opening a wall once, for both, is the point.

Save water

Overhead tank and sump on one line. The pump stops when the tank is full. It does not run a dry sump. RO and UV sized for the people who live there.

Save energy

Backup on the circuits you named, not on everything by accident. Solar where the roof, the shade, and the bill agree. Rooms bright only when someone needs them bright.
